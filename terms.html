Membership and accounts

  • Give accurate information and keep your password private. You're responsible for activity on your account.
  • We may suspend or remove accounts that are misused or that break these terms.

Dues

  • Membership dues are the amount and schedule shown in the member portal when you pay (for example, $25 per month). We may change dues for future payments; any change applies to new payments and new autopay sign-ups.
  • Autopay charges your card on the same day each billing period until you cancel. You can cancel any time from "Manage Autopay & Card" in the member portal; cancelling stops future charges and you keep the time you've already paid for.
  • Paying ahead covers the number of months (or weeks) you choose, added to any time already paid for.
  • Refunds are covered by our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
