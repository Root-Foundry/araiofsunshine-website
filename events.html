Celebrating our community and connections

Upcoming Events Calendar

Our monthly community meeting is the heart of A Rai of Sunshine. Families come together to share experiences, hear guest speakers, swap resources and build lasting friendships. We meet the last Sunday of each month at 3:00 PM in Monticello, and all families and individuals affected by disability are welcome.

The calendar also lists workshops, trainings and special events. Click on any event to see more details.
