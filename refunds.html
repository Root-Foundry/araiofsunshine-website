Refund & Cancellation Policy

Cancelling autopay, and when dues and donations can be refunded

Draft, October 1, 2026. This page was drafted for the website launch and must be reviewed by A Rai of Sunshine (and ideally its attorney) before it is published. Remove this note once approved.

We want paying dues and giving to be simple and fair. Here's how cancellations and refunds work.

Cancelling autopay

  • You can cancel autopay any time from Manage Autopay & Card in the member portal.
  • Cancelling stops future charges. Your membership stays active through the end of the period you've already paid for.

Membership dues

  • Dues are generally non-refundable, including time paid in advance.
  • If you were charged by mistake or charged twice, email us within 30 days and we'll refund the incorrect charge.
  • If a dues payment is refunded, the membership time it paid for is removed from your account.

Donations

  • Donations are generally non-refundable, because they go to work right away for families we serve.
  • If you gave the wrong amount or gave by mistake, email us within 30 days and we'll refund it.
  • Monthly gifts can be changed or cancelled any time using the "Manage or cancel your monthly gift" link on our Donate page, or by emailing us.

How to ask for a refund

Email araiofsunshineofnorthflorida@gmail.com with your name, the date and the amount. Approved refunds go back to the original card or bank account, usually within 5–10 business days.
