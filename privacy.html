Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information

Draft, October 1, 2026. This page was drafted for the website launch and must be reviewed by A Rai of Sunshine (and ideally its attorney) before it is published. Remove this note once approved.

A Rai of Sunshine of North Florida Inc. ("A Rai of Sunshine", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information our website collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Member accounts: your name, email, phone number, password (stored securely by our login provider, never visible to us), how you describe yourself ("I am a…"), your areas of interest and your email preferences.
  • Business listings: the business details, logo, address, hours and dates you submit for the member directory.
  • Payments and donations: the amount, date and status of dues payments and donations, and the name, email and any tribute note you give with a donation.
  • Website use: basic, anonymous information about how pages are visited (such as pages viewed and browser type), collected by our website analytics.

Card and bank details

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card or bank details go directly to Stripe and are never stored on our website or seen by us.

How we use your information

  • To run your membership: process dues, show your payment history and send dues reminders and receipts.
  • To publish business listings you submit. In the public directory we show only the details you enter for your listing; if you choose, your street address is hidden and only your city and state appear.
  • To send news, events and updates, if you've asked to receive them. You can turn these off any time in your member account. We'll still email you about your dues and account.
  • To acknowledge donations, including year-end tax acknowledgments.

Who can see it

Your account and payment information is visible only to you and to our authorized volunteers and staff who manage memberships. We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with the services that run the website (Google Firebase for hosting and accounts, Stripe for payments and our email provider for messages), or when required by law.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep your information while you're a member and as needed for our records. You can update most details in your member account. To have your account and listing deleted, email us at araiofsunshineofnorthflorida@gmail.com. Payment records kept by Stripe follow Stripe's own retention rules.

Children

Member accounts and donations are for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with its date. Questions? Email araiofsunshineofnorthflorida@gmail.com or write to us at 805 N. Jefferson Street, Unit 805, Monticello, FL.
